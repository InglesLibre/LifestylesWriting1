Modern Lifestyles and Health

B2 Essay Writing Practice

Instructions: Write an essay of 140–190 words in response to the prompt below.

  • Include an introduction and a conclusion.
  • Address all three points in the notes.
  • Use formal, academic language.
  • You have 40 minutes to complete this task.
Essay Prompt
"Modern lifestyles can seriously endanger our health."

Do you agree?

Write about:

  • food
  • physical activity
  • …… (your own choice)

Write your essay using all the notes.

Model Essay (164 words)

It is clear that modern lifestyles present serious risks to our health, and I strongly agree with this view. This essay will examine three areas where these dangers are most evident.

Firstly, the food we eat today is a major concern. Many people rely on fast food and processed meals because they are quick and convenient. However, these foods are often high in fat, sugar and salt, which can lead to obesity and heart disease over time.

Secondly, our lives have become increasingly sedentary. Most people spend hours sitting at desks or in front of screens, with little time for exercise. This lack of physical activity contributes to a range of health problems, including back pain and poor circulation.

Thirdly, modern life is often stressful. The pressure to succeed at work, combined with constant use of technology, leaves many people feeling exhausted. Chronic stress can weaken the immune system and cause mental health issues such as anxiety.

In conclusion, I believe that modern lifestyles do seriously endanger our health. It is important for individuals to make conscious choices about their diet, activity levels, and stress management to protect their well-being.
